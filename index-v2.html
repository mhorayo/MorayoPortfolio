BroadMoney · Fintech · 2024 — Present

Compliance that feels like care

Nearly 55% of new users quit before their first transfer, most of them at KYC. I split verification into tiers, so people can start sending small amounts and verify more only when they need to.

The core flow

The rate before the amount

The send flow answers what users care about in order: how much the recipient gets, who receives it, and what it costs. The live exchange rate shows before anyone types a number.

The result

Drop-off fell from 55% to 22%

New users made 2.3× as many transactions in their first month. Read the case study
