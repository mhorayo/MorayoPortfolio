Tax Clarity

Community project
2026Product design
Civic tech · Web

A free web platform that turns Nigeria's 2026 tax reforms into a plain-language, personal answer for freelancers, small business owners and salaried workers.

Three questions replace the legal text: income type, income range and business registration. The result says what changed, what to do next, and when you're clear.
