OwnIt
2026Product design · Founder
Creator SaaS · Web

Photographers, designers and filmmakers often have to send their work before they are paid, then hope the client pays.

OwnIt shares watermarked, expiring preview links and unlocks the clean files only after the client pays through Paystack. I designed the brand, the design system and the web app, first built for The Fusion Hack 2026.

01.Your work. Your terms.
02.One project, one page

Watermarked previews, locked files, link expiry and what you’ll receive after fees.

03.Payments

Clients pay through Paystack straight to the creative’s bank. OwnIt never holds the money.

Where it is now

A live, clickable prototype.

The messaging stays honest: previews are capture-resistant and traceable, never “screenshot-proof”.
