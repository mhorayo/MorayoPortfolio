Overview

One-stop payment infrastructure that serves both merchants and consumers

Help2Pay is a payment solution serving multiple markets — Nigeria, UK, and beyond. Merchants need dashboards to manage transactions, track settlements, and view analytics. End users need a mobile app for transfers, gift cards, shopping, and bill payments. I designed both sides of this system.

The challenge was making complex multi-currency routing, compliance checks, and settlement processes feel invisible — so merchants focus on their business and consumers focus on what they're buying, not on payment plumbing.

Reflection

Challenges and lessons

Two audiences, one system

Merchants need data density and operational control. End users need simplicity and speed. Designing both under one brand identity required careful separation of concerns.

Making compliance invisible

Multi-currency payments involve regulatory checks at every step. The design had to make these feel routine, not bureaucratic.
