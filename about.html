Hi, I’m Morayo

I design fintech and AI products that people trust with their money and their time.

I studied computer engineering, but I was always the one obsessing over how things look and feel. After university I taught myself Webflow, then Figma, designed for a fashion e-commerce brand, and took Google’s UX courses. Then came fintech: remittance, digital banking, document automation and AI.

It clicked when I started watching analytics: real people moving from screen A to screen B exactly the way I’d imagined. Design stopped being decoration and became proof.

I love order, routine and beautiful things. Design is how I see the world.
