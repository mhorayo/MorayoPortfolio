Hi, I’m Morayo

Tools change every year. People don’t. So I start with people.

I’m a product designer in Lagos. Before I open Figma, I want to understand the person on the other side of the screen: what they’re trying to get done, what worries them, and what they’ll never say out loud in an interview.

So I watch and I ask. I interview, sit in on moderated usability tests, read session recordings and heatmaps, and map journeys until the real problem shows up. Design thinking is a habit for me, not a workshop: understand, define, sketch on paper, prototype, test, and go again.

I also build with AI. I vibe code working prototypes with Claude and v0, explore ideas in Google Stitch, and ship clickable products, like the live OwnIt prototype, so a team can react to something real instead of a static mock.

Outside work, I’m a big fan of 3D animation and 3D animated films. I’ll rewatch one just to study how a character moves. That love of motion is why my work moves too.

Users in the middle. Everything else orbits.
